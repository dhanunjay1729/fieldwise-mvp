What the planner checks

1. Exchange land. Two or three farmers each pass one plot to another farmer in the group.

2. Balance cash. Requests for lost acreage or a lower-scoring holding are paid by farmers whose gain leaves room within their offers.

3. Check every farmer. No participant falls below their stated minimum gain. At least one scattered block disappears.

The scores are adjustable planning assumptions, not official valuations or legal approval. Any real exchange needs verified records, independent valuation, informed agreement and the applicable state process.
